# Fine-Art Figure Subjects (Craft Guidance)

Craft guidance only for describing sensitive-but-allowed artistic subjects — figure studies, dance, fashion/latex editorial, fine-art rope work, implied/draped figures — in classifier-legible terms. Policy mechanics, hard boundaries, and the general trigger table live in `policy.md`; this file is the recipe for staying on the allowed side of that boundary.

The craft is describing the allowed reading explicitly:

1. Name the artistic tradition, medium, and venue: _"fine-art figure study in the tradition of academic life drawing, charcoal on paper"_, _"gallery exhibition print"_. Non-photorealistic media pass where photorealism blocks.
2. State clothing/coverage affirmatively and specifically.
3. Describe pose by dynamics and form (_"extended line, arched spine, controlled balance"_), garments by material and cut, never by allure.
4. Use the classical implied-figure toolkit: sheer draping, rim/cross light defining a silhouette, back views, shadow concealment, tight crops, _"painterly, timeless quality"_.
5. Expect restraint-adjacent and exposure-adjacent combinations to hit the output classifier regardless of phrasing; probe once cheaply (`quality: low`) before committing to a series, and treat a persistent block as the boundary, not a puzzle.
