# Painterly / Oil / Impressionist

For journal covers and fine-art work.

- Name the movement or school explicitly: "Impressionist", "Hudson River School", "mid-19th century American landscape painting". Avoid living artists by name (see `policy.md`) — use movement/era/described aesthetic instead.
- Specify physical-medium qualities: _visible brushstrokes, impasto texture, palette-knife energy, canvas weave, no smooth blending, oil-paint luminosity_.
- Anchor palettes with named pigments: _sapphire blue, viridian green, carmine red, golden ochre_.
- Covers: _"editorial magazine cover layout"_, negative space reserved for title text, wide atmospheric scene. 2560×1440 works (either generatable model); larger is experimental.
- Series (e.g. a journal-cover run): reuse the established vocabulary verbatim (medium, lighting, palette words) across the series and vary only the declared subject — see `core.md`'s "Series consistency".
