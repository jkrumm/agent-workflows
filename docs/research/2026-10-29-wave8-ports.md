# Wave 8 ports: eval sets and pre-registered decision rules

**Status:** pre-registered 2026-10-29, before any comparison run. Results are appended under §Results; the rules
below are not edited after the first live run (a changed rule is a new section with its reason).

Concept §11 P4: the audio pipeline and its TTS/STT door, image and podcast; a caller switches only after its rule
passes. Eval cases are private (`$AGW_EVAL_DIR/<workflow>/cases.jsonl`, never in git). Eval runs and the one episode
use the team key; deploy verification the personal key. Legacy arms run on audio-gateway and image-gateway as
deployed. **Every rule names its aggregation** (Wave 7 retro): "per case" means every case must hold, "mean" or
"median" means over cases, "total" means summed over runs.

## image

- **Cases:** 8: blog header, icon source, OG image, a photo edit with an input image, two more generations of the
  kinds `/img` names, two cutouts of generated images.
- **Arms:** L = `imgcli gen|cutout --json` (image-gateway), N = `agw image`, 1 run per case each, same model, size,
  quality (`low` for generations) and `n`.
- **Scores (deterministic):** `valid` (PNG magic bytes, decodes, dimensions = the snapped requested size, `n` images;
  cutout: input dimensions), `alpha` (cutout only: an alpha channel with ≥ 5 % of pixels below 255 and ≥ 5 % at
  255), `priced` (a non-null cost > 0 in the run record), wall.
- **Rule:** N passes if (1) `valid` holds per case, 8/8, and `alpha` on both cutouts; (2) the request parameters N
  sent equal L's per case (model, size after snapping, quality, `n`); (3) per case N cost ≤ 1.10 × L cost where both
  are priced, and N priced on 8/8; (4) median wall(N) ≤ 1.25 × median wall(L) over cases. No visual judgement: the
  same model with the same parameters is the same generator.

## speech (TTS door, OpenAI-compatible `/v1/audio/speech`)

- **Cases:** 10 texts: 4 Hermes chat replies (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`, mp3), 2 streaming chat replies (same
  model, `response_format: pcm`), 2 briefings (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, the prep lane, mp3, title header), 1
  ElevenLabs (`elevenlabs/flash-v2.5`, mp3), 1 with a `delivery` key and inline tags (lite model, wav).
- **Arms:** L = audio-gateway (VPS, as Hermes calls it today), N = the audio process's door; 1 run per case, the two
  pcm cases 3 runs each per arm (time to first byte).
- **Scores:** `valid` (ffprobe decodes the body; container matches `response_format`; pcm and wav are s16le mono
  24 kHz), duration, `title` (header present when prep ran), time to first byte (pcm), cost, `intelligible` (N's
  own STT door transcribes N's mp3 of the 4 chat cases; agreement = 1 − WER against the input text after
  normalisation: lowercase, punctuation and inline tags stripped, whitespace collapsed).
- **Rule:** N passes if (1) `valid` per case, 10/10; (2) duration(N)/duration(L) in [0.75, 1.33] per case; (3)
  `title` present on both prep cases; (4) median time to first byte over the 6 pcm runs: N ≤ L + 300 ms; (5) total
  cost(N) within ±10 % of total cost(L), every N call priced; (6) mean `intelligible` over the 4 chat cases ≥ 0.90.

## transcriptions (STT door, OpenAI-compatible `/v1/audio/transcriptions`)

- **Cases:** 10 recordings from Hermes' voice inputs on the mini (private; German and English): at least 2 longer
  than 240 s (the chunking path) and 1 above 25 MiB or 20 min (built by concatenating recordings if none exists).
  *Amended 2026-10-29, before any run:* Hermes keeps no voice inputs on disk (its caches hold six audio files, none a
  voice input), so the 10 cases are those six files, slices and concatenations of them, and slices of legacy podcast
  episodes and lab recordings (German two-host speech); at least 3 English cases. Same thresholds.
  Default model and `response_format: json`; 4 extra calls on the shortest case for `text`, `srt`, `vtt`,
  `verbose_json`.
- **Arms:** L = audio-gateway (VPS) run twice per case (L, L′: the reproducibility baseline, Wave 5's lesson), N =
  the audio process's door, once per case.
- **Scores:** agreement(a, b) = 1 − WER after the normalisation above; `format` (srt/vtt cues parse with monotone
  timestamps, verbose_json has segments with monotone `start`/`end`, text equals json's `text`); cost.
- **Rule:** N passes if (1) mean agreement(N, L) ≥ mean agreement(L, L′) − 0.02 over cases; (2) agreement(N, L) ≥
  0.85 per case; (3) `format` 4/4; (4) total cost(N) within ±10 % of total cost(L), every N call priced.

## podcast

- **Offline parity (free, on the 20 recorded legacy jobs in audio-gateway's ledger):** (1) the ported lint
  (`podcast-lint`) reports exactly legacy's findings per script, 20/20 (per script, every counter equal; legacy's
  module imported from the audio-gateway checkout); (2) the ported multi-speaker segmentation
  (`unitsForMultiSpeaker`) yields legacy's units per script, 20/20 (exact).
- **The episode (one, end to end on the new path):** a real brain note as source, `--publish`, cover and brain note
  on, research on, the request's `minutes` 15.
  - (3) The run reaches `done`: an MP3 with one chapter per segment and an embedded cover, published to
    Audiobookshelf (the library item is readable through its API), the brain note committed and pushed.
  - (4) Integrated loudness (ffmpeg `ebur128`) within ±1 LU of the median of the 19 legacy episode MP3s.
  - (5) |duration / requested minutes − 1| ≤ 0.29, legacy's 75th percentile over its last 10 done episodes (median
    0.22; computed 2026-10-29 from the ledger's `durationSeconds` and request `minutes`).
  - (6) Script lint: findings per 10 turns ≤ the 75th percentile of the last 10 legacy episodes (total findings,
    one number per episode, computed with the ported lint on their recorded scripts).
  - (7) Cost per minute of audio, research excluded (legacy's `costUsd` excludes research), ≤ 1.25 × legacy's
    median over its last 10 done episodes ($0.2125/min, so ≤ $0.266/min); every call priced; research spend
    reported separately.
  - (8) Durability: the episode survives one restart of the audio process at a stage boundary after the script
    exists; the run resumes and no completed step runs twice (journal: one attempt per done step).
- If (3)–(8) fail, `/podcast` does not switch; the lead reports with the numbers. A second episode for a failed
  criterion needs its own rule section first.
